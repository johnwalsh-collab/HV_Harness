Caspase family in Cyprinus carpio

A nested view of the curated Cyprinus carpio Caspase family inventory, organised around homeolog slots — the ancestral pre-allopolyploidization positions whose A and B copies should match.

Source: results/identification/Cyprinus_carpio_caspase_curation_data.json (AI-assisted, synteny-based curation).

Homeolog slots: each homeolog pair is broken into a list of "slots" — one per ancestral pre-allopolyploidization gene at that locus. Each slot shows what the A and B subgenomes have retained at that position. An empty cell labelled absent — no specific search done means the gene is not in the annotation but the locus has not been actively investigated; candidate loss · annotation-level is the strongest claim made anywhere in this view, reserved for cases where the curation actively searched the GFF for any group-related feature in the syntenic region and found none. Sequence-level loss confirmation is treated as a downstream side project beyond the core workflow scope.

Standard of evidence: bring clarity but maintain humility. Show what the annotation-level data supports; do not claim more.

Identity convention: where NCBI's gene name conflicts with the synteny-derived identity, the synteny call is shown as the role and the NCBI name appears in metadata.
